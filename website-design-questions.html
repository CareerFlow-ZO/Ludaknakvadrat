BUSINESS WEBSITE GUIDE

Website design: questions and answers

Practical answers about LNK DIGITAL services, project planning, redesigns and SEO-ready foundations.

What does LNK DIGITAL do?

LNK DIGITAL designs and develops business websites, website redesigns, landing pages and SEO-ready page structures for companies in Europe and the United States.

Does LNK DIGITAL work with clients outside Slovenia?

Yes. LNK DIGITAL works remotely with businesses in Slovenia, Austria, Germany, Switzerland, Croatia, Italy and the United States.

Can LNK DIGITAL redesign an existing website?

Yes. The redesign process reviews the existing website, improves its content structure and mobile experience, and protects useful URLs where feasible.

Does a new website include SEO?

Projects can include SEO foundations such as metadata, canonical URLs, internal linking and sitemap structure. The exact scope is confirmed for each project.

How do I request a website preview?

Send your current website URL, or your business name, services and target market. LNK DIGITAL can then discuss a suitable visual preview before a full build is agreed.

Can LNK DIGITAL build a multilingual website?

Yes. Multilingual-ready websites and market-specific content structures are available depending on the project scope.

Does LNK DIGITAL guarantee first place in Google or AI answers?

No. Search engines and AI services decide which sources to show. Technical SEO and clear, useful content can improve eligibility and discoverability, but placement is not guaranteed.
