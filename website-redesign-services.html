Frequently asked questions

Do I need a full rebuild?

Not always. We first assess whether the current platform and content can support a targeted redesign.

Will a redesign hurt existing Google visibility?

Poorly planned URL changes can hurt visibility. We plan redirects and preserve important URLs where possible, but rankings can still change after a redesign.

Can I see a redesign direction first?

For suitable projects, yes. Send the current URL and we can prepare a visual direction before the full scope is agreed.
