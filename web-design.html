Frequently asked questions

Can LNK DIGITAL work with clients outside Slovenia?

Yes. Discovery, content handoff, feedback and approvals can be handled remotely for clients in Europe and the United States.

Do you guarantee a specific Google ranking?

No. We build sound technical and on-page foundations, but search rankings depend on competition, authority, content, location and many other factors.

Can you move the site to a new domain later?

Yes. When a new LNK DIGITAL domain is ready, the migration can be planned with canonical updates and permanent redirects from relevant old URLs.
